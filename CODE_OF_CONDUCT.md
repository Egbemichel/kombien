# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Kombien is a community project about making a shared, everyday experience — negotiating a taxi fare — easier for everyone. That spirit extends to how we treat each other here. We're building this in the open, across cultures and experience levels, and we want it to stay a welcoming place to contribute.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints, experience levels, and cultural context — this project is rooted in a specific Cameroonian experience, and contributors from other backgrounds are asked to engage with that context in good faith rather than flattening it.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus criticism on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask questions before assuming bad faith.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, public or private.
- Discriminatory jokes or language, including mocking passengers, drivers, or cultural stereotypes tied to the project's subject matter.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or reviews.

## Scope

This applies within all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other official communication channels — and in public spaces when someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it by opening a private communication with the maintainers (see [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) for who that currently is), or via GitHub's built-in reporting tools if you're not comfortable contacting maintainers directly. Reports will be handled discreetly.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code of conduct, and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate it. See [GOVERNANCE.md#moderation](GOVERNANCE.md#moderation) for the concrete escalation path (warning → content moderation/block → appeal).
