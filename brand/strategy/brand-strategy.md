# Brand Strategy

**Status: approved.** This is the founding strategy direction — it can still evolve as the project and its community grow, but it's the working reference now, not a draft.

## Purpose

Kombien turns everyday taxi fare experiences into shared public knowledge so passengers can make more informed decisions before negotiating their next ride.

## Functional promise

Users can explore reported taxi fares for routes and contribute the fare they paid after a trip.

## Emotional promise

> "I do not have to enter the next taxi negotiation completely blind."

## Cultural insight

In Cameroon, taxi fare negotiation is often a short public exchange involving a destination and a proposed price:

- "Poste Centrale, 250."
- "Mvan, 500."
- "Bonamoussadi, 500."
- "Destination, price." / "Price, destination."

Passengers may stand by the roadside for several minutes or much longer, call out their destination and fare, get rejected, counteroffer, or overpay because they lack reliable reference information. Kombien recognizes this experience without mocking passengers or drivers — see [cultural-context.md](cultural-context.md) for the full treatment.

## Brand personality

- Street-smart
- Direct
- Witty
- Grounded
- Transparent
- Community-oriented
- Open to contribution
- Technically precise

## What the brand should avoid

- Generic mobility-startup language
- Corporate buzzwords
- Overclaiming fare accuracy
- Treating one reported fare as universal truth
- Mocking drivers or passengers
- Excessive cultural stereotypes
- Generic African visual clichés
- Taxi silhouettes, map pins, steering wheels, standard road graphics
- Unnecessary AI branding
- Excessive gradients or visual clutter

## How this shows up

- Product copy states ranges and recency, not single "correct" prices (see [functional promise](#functional-promise) and the eventual product UI guidance).
- Visual identity leans on typography and language, not literal transportation iconography (see [../logo/README.md](../logo/README.md)).
- Voice is direct and a little witty, never corporate — see [../voice/voice-and-tone.md](../voice/voice-and-tone.md).
