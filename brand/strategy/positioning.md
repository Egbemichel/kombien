## What Kombien is not

- Not a ride-hailing app — it doesn't book, dispatch, or facilitate rides.
- Not a fare-fixing authority — it reports observed community data, not an official or enforced price.
- Not a generic "mobility platform" — it is specifically about the negotiation moment, not fleet management, logistics, or route optimization.

## Competitive/reference framing

Kombien sits closer to community-data projects (crowdsourced pricing, OpenStreetMap-style contribution models) than to ride-hailing apps (Uber, Bolt) or fare calculators built by a single authority. Evaluate it against the former, not the latter, when making brand and product decisions.
