# Voice Do's and Don'ts

**Status: approved quick reference.** Full detail in [voice-and-tone.md](voice-and-tone.md).

## Do

- State ranges and recency, not single "correct" prices.
- Say plainly when data is thin.
- Keep copy short and direct.
- Let humor come from shared familiarity with the negotiation, not mockery.
- Use Franglish where already approved (see [franglais-guidance.md](franglais-guidance.md)).

## Don't

- Don't claim a fare is "official" or "verified" unless it genuinely is.
- Don't use generic mobility-startup language ("optimize your journey," "seamless experience").
- Don't mock drivers or passengers, even lightly.
- Don't lean on stereotypes for humor.
- Don't guilt-trip users into contributing data.
- Don't overuse the tagline as filler copy — it's approved for use, but it's a tagline, not a sentence to repeat throughout the UI.
