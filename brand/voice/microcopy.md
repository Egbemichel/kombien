# Microcopy Patterns

**Status: approved patterns.** These are the patterns to follow once product UI exists — no screens have been built yet, so treat the examples below as illustrative of the *pattern*, not literally shipped strings.

## Fare display

- Prefer ranges over single numbers: `400–600 FCFA` rather than `500 FCFA`.
- Always show recency and volume when available: `(12 reports, last one 3 days ago)`.
- When data is thin, say so instead of hiding it: `Only 2 reports so far — could be off.`

## Contribution prompts

- Keep the ask small: `What did you pay for this ride?`
- Confirm without over-celebrating: `Thanks — added to Mvan → Poste Centrale.`
- Never guilt-trip non-contributors. No "help the community!" pressure copy.

## Empty states

- Be honest, not apologetic: `No reports yet for this route. Be the first.`
- Avoid generic empty-state clichés ("Nothing here yet! 🎉") — keep it plain and in-voice.

## Errors

- State what happened and what to do: `Couldn't find that route. Try a nearby landmark instead.`
- Never blame the user's input phrasing as "wrong."

## Buttons and labels

- Verb-first, short: `Report a fare`, `Search a route`, `See recent reports`.
- Avoid jargon like "submit," "CTA"-speak, or "optimize."

## Franglish in microcopy

See [franglais-guidance.md](franglais-guidance.md) for exactly where mixed French/English phrasing belongs by default (cultural/community-facing copy) versus where plain locale-appropriate language takes over (functional strings like buttons and errors).
