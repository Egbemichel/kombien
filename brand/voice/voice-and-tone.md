# Voice and Tone

**Status: approved.**

## Voice (consistent across contexts)

Kombien's voice is:

- **Street-smart** — it knows how the negotiation actually works, not a textbook version of it.
- **Direct** — says what it means in as few words as needed. States a fare range, not a paragraph of caveats.
- **Witty** — dry, observational humor is welcome; jokes at anyone's expense are not.
- **Grounded** — no hype, no overclaiming accuracy.
- **Transparent** — plain about what it doesn't know (thin data, stale reports, wide ranges).
- **Community-oriented** — talks about "reported" and "shared" data, not "our" data or "verified" prices.

## Tone (shifts by context)

| Context | Tone |
|---|---|
| Fare results | Plain, factual, confident about what it knows, clear about what it doesn't |
| Empty states / thin data | Honest, a little warm — "not much data yet for this route" beats a fake-confident number |
| Contribution flow | Encouraging, low-friction — make reporting a fare feel quick and worthwhile |
| Documentation (this repo) | Practical and welcoming, per [../../docs/setup/github-setup.md](../../docs/setup/github-setup.md) |
| Errors | Direct, no blame, clear next step |

## Examples

**Good:**
> "Reported fares for Mvan → Poste Centrale: 400–600 FCFA (12 reports, last one 3 days ago)."

**Avoid (overclaiming):**
> "The official fare for this route is 500 FCFA."

**Good (thin data):**
> "Only 2 reports for this route so far — could be off. Add yours after your ride?"

**Avoid (corporate):**
> "We are leveraging community insights to optimize your mobility experience."
