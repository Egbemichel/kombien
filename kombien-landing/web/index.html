Get Kombien

No store.
Just your browser.

Open Kombien, then add it to your home screen. It opens like any other app, and it keeps working offline.

Android

  1. Open Kombien in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install, or ⋮ then Install app.
  3. Open it from your home screen.

iPhone

  1. Open Kombien in Safari.
  2. Tap Share.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
