Privacy Policy

Version 0.7 · Last updated 13 November 2026

In short: no account, no name, no phone number. A fare report is a route, a price and a few optional details. Your location helps find your starting point and is never stored with a report. Only averages for a route are ever shown, and only once enough riders reported it. No ads, no tracking, nothing sold.

Who we are

Kombien is an open-source app that shows riders in Cameroon what other riders recently paid for a taxi or moto route. It is made by Egbe Michel. The code is public at github.com/Egbemichel/kombien.

What Kombien collects

  • Fare reports you choose to send: the start and end landmarks, the amount you paid, the part of the day, and, if you add them, the weather and the vehicle (taxi or moto), with the time of the report.
  • An anonymous ID for your phone: created the first time you send a report. It is not linked to your name, number or any account. It exists only to stop spam and false reports, never to follow anyone.
  • A device hint with each report: a scrambled code made from your screen size, pixel density, time zone, system and language. It cannot tell who you are and is never shown. When a report arrives, the server also turns the rough neighbourhood of your network address into a second scrambled code that changes every day; your address itself is never saved. The codes are only compared with those on other reports, so that one person using several browsers on one phone counts once towards a route's fare range.
  • Your location, only if you allow it: used on your phone to set your exact starting point. It may be sent with a landmark search so results come nearest first. It is not stored with your reports and not kept as a history of where you go.
  • Problem reports and ratings you choose to send: what went wrong, where in the app, an optional note, and the app version and language; and the thumbs up or down you give a fare range.
  • Searches that find nothing: when a place you search for isn't in Kombien, the words you typed are counted, with the city and your language, so we can add the places riders are missing. Nothing says who typed them: no ID, no location. The same words from many riders are added into one count. Anything that looks like a phone number, an email address or a link is never sent.
  • Places you look for that Kombien doesn't have yet: if a place isn't in Kombien, the words you typed are sent to Google Places so it can suggest matches. Google also receives your device's IP address, as with any request you make to a website. No Kombien ID, name, number or location is sent. If you pick a suggestion, we keep only Google's code for that place (its ID) and the name you gave it, so other riders can find the place and see fares for it. We do not keep Google's name for the place.
  • Places you add yourself: the name you give a place and where it is: your phone's position at that moment if you choose "I'm here now", or a known place it is near and about how far. The place then appears for every rider, so they can find it and see fares for it. We keep an anonymous ID with each addition, only to limit abuse; it is never shown to other riders.
  • Notifications, only if you turn them on: the address your browser gives for sending notifications to this phone, its encryption keys, your language and the topics you chose. Nothing else about you, and never your location. With notifications on you may hear when your report unlocks fares, when Kombien has been updated, and get a short reminder at about 08:30 every three days (never at night, and at most one notification a day). Turning notifications off in Settings deletes it.
  • Technical records: like any website, our hosting providers briefly keep standard request logs (for example the IP address of a request) for security and to keep the service running.

What stays on your phone

Your language, saved places (Home, School, Work…), settings, the list of reports you made, and saved fares for offline use are kept on your phone only. You can remove them at any time: delete your reports in Settings → My reports, or clear Kombien's data in your browser or phone settings.

What we show to others

Only aggregated fares: a range for a route, built from recent reports. A range appears only once at least 3 riders reported that route, so a single trip can never be seen or traced back to someone.

What we never do

  • No advertising and no advertising trackers.
  • No selling or renting of data to anyone.
  • No attempt to identify who sent a report.
  • No use of your data beyond showing community fare information.

Who handles the data

Kombien is hosted by Cloudflare (the website) and Supabase (the database). They process data only to run the service for us, under their own security and privacy commitments. Google Places is used only for the place suggestions described above, and only when a place isn't in Kombien; Google's own privacy policy applies to what it receives.

How long we keep it

Fare reports are kept so route ranges stay useful over time; recent reports (the last 90 days) count most. Notification addresses are kept until you turn notifications off, or until your browser says they no longer work. Request logs at our providers are short-lived.

Your choices and rights

  • Use Kombien without sharing your location: search a landmark instead.
  • Turn notifications on or off at any time in Settings.
  • Delete the reports kept on your phone from Settings → My reports.
  • Ask us to delete the reports your phone sent to the shared data, or ask any question about your data, using the contact below.

Children

Kombien is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app will tell you and ask you to accept the new version. The date at the top always shows the latest update.

Contact
