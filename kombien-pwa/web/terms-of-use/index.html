In short: Kombien shows what other riders say they paid. It is a guide to help you negotiate, not an official or guaranteed price. Report honestly, and don't misuse the service.

1. Using Kombien

By using Kombien you agree to these terms and to the Privacy Policy. You don't need an account. If you don't agree, please don't use the service.

2. Fares are guidance, not a promise

Fare ranges are built from reports sent by riders. They are not official fares, may be incomplete or out of date, and can differ from what a driver asks. The fare you agree is always between you and the driver. Kombien is not a transport service and is not part of that agreement.

3. Reporting fares

  • Only report fares you actually paid, as accurately as you can.
  • Don't send false, misleading or automated reports, and don't try to push a route's range up or down.
  • Reports that look abusive can be removed, and a phone that keeps sending them can be blocked from reporting.

4. Your reports

When you send a fare report, you allow Kombien to store it, combine it with other reports and publish the resulting ranges, for free and without time limit. Reports are never shown on their own or with anything that identifies you.

5. Acceptable use

Don't attack, overload, reverse-engineer for abuse, or scrape the service in a way that harms it or its riders, and don't use it to break the law.
